# SHAPE.md

The physical device. For the hackathon this exists only as Blender renders plus the web-app twin.

## Form

- Flip-style handheld, smaller than a phone.
- One screen, physical buttons, strong haptic and touch feedback.
- Design and feel are core to the product, not extras.
- Exact size, screen and battery: TBD.

## Modes

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Handheld | Off the dock, on battery. Voice notes, talking to agents, approving actions. |
| Docked | Screen-only mode beside a desktop. Companion display: focus/Pomodoro timer, todos, Claude limit reset countdown. |

## Buttons

- **Approve:** a physical press with a haptic click confirms risky agent actions (send, delete, pay, anything leaving your machines). This is the main reason the hardware has buttons.
- **Talk:** press to start voice input.
- Exact layout: TBD.

## Dock

- Magnetic cradle or puck with a charging light.
- In the demo it's a second screen (laptop tab) showing synced tasks and history.

## Future real hardware

- Board: Raspberry Pi 5-class (arm64).
- Mic, speaker, small screen, buttons, battery.
- Firmware could later move to Rust or C++ on something like an ESP32-S3 speaking the same protocol.
