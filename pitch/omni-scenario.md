# Omni — From a thought to a team

**Format:** 16:9. All dialogue, captions, task titles, and screen text are in English.

**Timing:** approximately 100 seconds for the full version. The earlier 65-second version is a compact edit target. The revised brief adds note browsing, several control close-ups, the sliding panel, and a new spoken ending. The full timing below gives those actions room to be understood. Keep every story beat when planning a shorter edit; shorten transitions and pauses first.

**Story:** the person opens a saved idea for a money-management app. The Notes agent starts a tracker on the connected PC. The To-Do agent gives the person a clear first action. The Focus agent helps them start that action while the other agents continue. A final new idea shows that Omni can form another specialist mini.

## Visual and character continuity

- **Omni / main bot:** white, round, with two upright black eyes. Calm, responsive, and slightly playful. Omni speaks directly to the person.
- **Notes agent:** warm yellow, with a soft square body and curious, slightly tilted eyes. It organizes the saved idea and starts the spreadsheet work.
- **To-Do agent:** blue, with a wide capsule body and alert eyes. It turns the idea into concrete actions.
- **Focus agent:** purple, with a rounded square body and relaxed eyes. It introduces the focus session, then leaves the timer clear.
- **New mini:** small and mint green, with a distinct face. It first appears inside the thought cloud. It represents the proposed app-building specialist.
