## Production notes

- Show the waveform and word transcription at the same time. Use short spoken phrases and readable subtitles.
- The Notes agent and its window leave the screen when the To-Do agent takes over. The tracker continues in the background and reappears as active work on the Agents screen.
- Keep each control and its result in the same shot: in-app wheel → 05:00; push bar → running timer; system wheel → Agents; red mic hold → speech and transcription.
- The fold is a proposed pose. Keep the display visible when the lower controls fold behind it. Verify the final hinge and Dock geometry before animation.
- Specialist agent roles, speech replies and PC spreadsheet work are storyboard concepts. They are not verified runtime behaviours.
- All supplied references remain unchanged. New story content is drawn over them in HTML.
