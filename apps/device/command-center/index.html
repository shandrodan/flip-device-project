Command center

Press C and the shade drops from the top. Spin the in-app wheel and the switches turn on a flywheel, round behind the screen. A flips the one in the centre. Notifications sit under the wheel. C again closes it.

I or C = C · W S or scroll on the screen = in-app wheel · arrows = D-pad · K or Enter = A · L or Esc = B · Earlier four directions
Wheel D-pad
  • Wheel forward and D-pad right both bring the right-hand switch to the centre; the one on the left slides up behind the screen and comes back round on the right. It never ends (in-app wheel rule).
  • D-pad left/right also spins it. D-pad down goes into the notifications, up comes back to the wheel. A on a notification opens it.
  • Turning Tailscale or Wi-Fi off makes the bot sad and tells you why. Battery saver turns the battery yellow.
